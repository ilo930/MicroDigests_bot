04 / Where it comes from

Named feeds, checkable links.

Stories come from named publisher feeds: Spaceflight Now, SpaceNews, NASA, ESA, JAXA, Ars Technica, Mining.com, The Quantum Insider, Phys.org and others, plus regional desks for India and Asia. A language model picks the seven most surprising and writes the three plain lines under each. It never invents a number.

Some newsrooms are paid for by a government. When a story comes from one, the item carries a small ⚠️ and that country's flag, on the same terms for the BBC, Deutsche Welle, CGTN and RT. It tells you who funds the reporting. What you make of that is yours to decide.

A lot is changing out there. Almost none of it reaches you.
