04

Where it comes from

Stories are pulled from named publisher feeds — Spaceflight Now, SpaceNews, NASA, ESA, JAXA, Ars Technica, Mining.com, The Quantum Insider, Phys.org and others — plus regional desks for India and Asia. A language model picks the seven most surprising and writes the three plain-English lines. It never invents a number: prices come from market data, and every story keeps its link so you can go and check.

Chinese and Russian state outlets are included on purpose, so their voice reaches you firsthand. Every such item is flagged ⚠️ in the digest, to be read with a pinch of salt.
